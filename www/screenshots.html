About these pictures

Every screenshot was taken on LikeOS itself, at 1920×1200 — the screen size a default build asks the firmware for. The newest pictures show the current development release, 0.2.7-HEAD (dev branch), whose WebKitGTK-based browser luakit runs today’s demanding websites; the older ones document the way there, from the first X.Org desktop in 0.2.4 to the GTK3 applications of 0.2.5.

Faster than in the video. Since the video was recorded, the GPU drivers have been extended considerably — among other things, the Intel graphics driver now runs the GPU at its full clock and hands work to it without needless waits. On the current 0.2.7-HEAD (dev branch), luakit and WebKitGTK load and scroll pages much faster than the video shows.
